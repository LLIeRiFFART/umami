About this website
Umami is a fictional restaurant website created as a portfolio project. Restaurant details, menu items and customer reviews are demonstration content. This website is not intended for placing real orders or reservations.

Contact forms
The callback form demonstrates a contact interface. Please do not enter real phone numbers or other personal information. Depending on the hosting configuration, submitting the form may send its contents to the configured server-side handler.

No accounts or public profiles
This website does not offer registration, user accounts, comments or public posting.

Third-party services

Hosting and external links
